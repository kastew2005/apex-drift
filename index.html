Add APEX DRIFT to your Home Screen

On your iPhone:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

The game will open from its own icon. Load it once while online so its files can be saved for offline use.
